Which action is regular? Which is temporary?

Find the time markers and the verb forms before opening the rule.

RULE · ПО-РУССКИ

Present Simple = обычное и повторяющееся.
Present Continuous = сейчас или временно.

Simple: I/you/we/they post; he/she posts. Continuous: am/is/are + verb-ing.

REAL EXAMPLE

I usually study at my desk, but this week I’m working in the library.

Обе части естественно соединяются через but, потому что показывают контраст.

COMMON MISTAKE

She working now. ✗
She is working now. ✓

Keks: “Almost. Check the auxiliary.”
